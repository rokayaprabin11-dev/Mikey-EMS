# Design Mikey's Finance Screens Around Nepal's Rules

Mikey English Medium School's Super Admin and Accountant screens rest on three Nepal-specific rules. **Bikram Sambat (BS) dates come first, with AD second.** **Rupee amounts use lakh grouping (Rs. 12,34,567.00).** **A posted receipt is never edited or deleted.** It can only be voided with a reason, and any reprint is marked as a copy. Today is **14 Kartik 2083 BS (Saturday, 31 Oct 2026)**. That falls inside **Academic Session 2083**, which began 15 Baishakh, and **Fiscal Year 2083/84**, which began 1 Shrawan. The app therefore needs both: a session for billing, and a fiscal year for receipt numbering and accounts. The law shapes the data model directly. Private-school fees are **limited to 14 legal headings**, admission is capped at one month's tuition and the annual fee at two. Education is **VAT-exempt**, so receipts show the school's PAN and no VAT line. For daily work, the fee counter should follow keyboard-first tools such as TallyPrime: one screen, live totals, and a single "Collect & Print" commit protected against double submission. Each day ends with a cash count by payment mode. On a two-PC LAN, "offline-first" should mean the app works without the internet. It should not mean the client keeps taking money when it cannot reach the office server. The browser cannot detect a server outage, so a heartbeat should drive the status indicator and block money writes. Biometric attendance needs three things: a raw-punch layer kept separate from computed attendance, an inbox for unmatched IDs, and rule settings with a visual timeline. Backups need a verified history and a heavily guarded restore. Several facts that change the design are still unconfirmed: Banepa's fee caps, the device brand, the receipt printer, and IRD software certification. These lead the client question list at the end.

## Bikram Sambat leads every date, and two years run side by side

Two independent sources agree that **31 October 2026 is 14 Kartik 2083 BS**. One is a panchang ([VedicMarga](https://vedicmarga.com/hindu-calendar/2026/10/31/)); the other is the `nepali-datetime` library run locally ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/nepali-datetime/)). In tables and date pickers, use a sortable numeric form such as **2083-07-14** or 2083/07/14. Nepali government notices use this style, for example "2082/12/22 (BS)" ([Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/post/school-fees-buddha-shanti-rural-municipality/)). Headers and receipts should spell the month out, with AD after it: **"14 Kartik 2083 (31 Oct 2026)"**. That matches the guidance to give both calendars on important documents and spell month names to avoid ambiguity ([MeroKalam](https://merokalam.com/blog/12-nepali-months-english-date-calendar/)). Month spellings vary (Asar/Ashadh, Poush/Push, Falgun/Phagun), so pick one set and use it everywhere.

The calendar has a hard engineering consequence. **BS months run 29 to 32 days, and the lengths change from year to year.** In 2083, Asar has 32 days and Mangsir has 29 ([nepali-datetime](https://pypi.org/project/nepali-datetime/)). Gregorian validation will reject valid dates. Date pickers must accept day 32, conversion must use a lookup table, and that table must be updatable for years beyond the library's range. Monthly attendance matrices likewise need up to 32 day columns. The 2083 table below is the reference for mockups and test data.

| BS month (2083) | Devanagari | Starts (AD) | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baishakh | बैशाख | 14 Apr 2026 | 31 |
| Jestha | जेठ | 15 May 2026 | 31 |
| Asar | असार | 15 Jun 2026 | **32** |
| Shrawan | साउन | 17 Jul 2026 | 31 |
| Bhadra | भदौ | 17 Aug 2026 | 31 |
| Ashwin | असोज | 17 Sep 2026 | 31 |
| Kartik | कात्तिक | 18 Oct 2026 | 30 |
| Mangsir | मंसिर | 17 Nov 2026 | 29 |

| Falgun | फागुन | 13 Feb 2027 | 30 |
| Chaitra | चैत | 15 Mar 2027 | 30 |

Two different years apply to the same transactions. The national **fiscal year 2083/84 began 1 Shrawan 2083 (17 Jul 2026)** ([Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/government-fully-waives-education-and-health-equality-fees/)). The school **session began 15 Baishakh 2083 (28 Apr 2026)**, moved from the earlier 2 Baishakh ([Ratopati](https://english.ratopati.com/story/60769/new-academic-session-starts-today)). Some valley municipalities started classes on 15 April instead ([Ratopati](https://english.ratopati.com/story/60128/mayors-forum-to-select-balen-admissions-from-april-11-and-decision-to-start-classes-from-april-15)), and districts such as Lamjung started on Baisakh 10 ([eKantipur](https://ekantipur.com/gandaki-pradesh/2026/04/19/en/schools-in-half-of-lamjung-municipalities-to-resume-classes-from-baisakh-10-32-34.html)). The session start date must therefore be configurable each year, not hard-coded. Because the Baishakh–Asar fees of Session 2083 belong to FY 2082/83 and the rest belong to FY 2083/84, **every finance report needs a "Session / Fiscal year" switch**. Receipt numbers should reset per fiscal year, in a form such as "2083/84-000123". Fees are billed by BS month: tuition is charged over 12 months, Baishakh to Chaitra.

## Money, fee heads and receipts follow Nepali law and print habits

### Lakh grouping and "Rs." are the everyday convention

Show amounts as **"Rs." with Indian-style grouping**: Rs. 12,34,567.00. `Intl.NumberFormat('en-IN')` produces this grouping. The CLDR `ne-NP` locale renders the symbol as "नेरू", which is technically correct but unfamiliar on English documents ([Unicode CLDR](https://cldr.unicode.org/)). Nepali English-language press writes "Rs2,400" ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/04/26/kathmandu-municipalities-cap-private-school-fees)). The official symbol is रु and the ISO code is NPR ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_rupee)). Use NPR only in exports, and रु. only for Devanagari output. Receipts and ledgers should show two decimals. KPI tiles can use whole rupees or compact lakh notation ("Rs 12.4L"). That follows the finance-UI rule of full figures in tables and compact figures on KPIs, with one shared formatter and a true minus sign (U+2212) for negatives ([Dash](https://brand.dash.fi/foundations/data-viz)). Make amount columns wide enough for "1,00,00,000.00". South Asian receipts conventionally add the **amount in words** with lakh/crore wording ending in "Only". I found no primary source for that wording, so match it to the school's current paper receipt.

### Fourteen legal fee headings become the fee-head list

The *Criteria for Fee Determination in Institutional Schools, 2072* allow **only 14 fee headings**. Charging any other heading, or above the limits, brings a fine of **up to Rs. 25,000 for a first violation and revocation of the permit for a second** ([eKantipur](https://ekantipur.com/Education/2026/04/06/under-what-headings-can-private-schools-charge-fees-13-47.html)). The fee-structure screen should offer only these headings, which are reconstructed from Kantipur's summary rather than the criteria document itself.

| # | Heading | Nepali | Legal limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly tuition | मासिक पढाइ शुल्क | Max 12 months/year |
| 2 | Annual fee | वार्षिक शुल्क | ≤ 2× monthly tuition (covers sports, ECA, lab, library, repairs, first aid) |
| 3 | Admission | भर्ना शुल्क | Once; ≤ 1× monthly tuition |
| 4 | Security deposit | धरौटी | Refundable |
| 5 | Exam | परीक्षा शुल्क | ≤ 50% of monthly tuition |
| 6 | Computer | कम्प्युटर | — |
| 7 | Transfer certificate | स्थानान्तरण प्रमाणपत्र | — |
| 8 | Special training | विशेष प्रशिक्षण | — |
| 9 | Hostel | आवास | Agreed with parents' assembly |
| 10 | Meals/snacks | भोजन / खाजा | Approved by user parents |
| 11 | Transport | यातायात | Approved by 2/3 of user parents |
| 12 | Educational tour | शैक्षिक भ्रमण | — |
| 13 | Inter-school competition | अन्तरविद्यालय प्रतियोगिता | — |
| 14 | Educational materials | शैक्षिक सामग्री | ≤ 10% of monthly tuition, once a year |

Three design rules follow. First, items such as "ECA", "Library" or "Lab" should appear as sub-lines under the Annual fee, not as their own heads. A "development charge" heading should not exist at all. Second, the fee-structure editor should **warn when admission exceeds 1× or annual exceeds 2× monthly tuition**, the multiples that the school umbrella bodies themselves describe ([Ratopati](https://english.ratopati.com/story/59377/private-school-administrators-say-baisakh-fees-must-be-paid)). Third, a late fine is not one of the 14 headings. It should be a configurable penalty line, like the 2.5%-on-balance charge one Kathmandu school uses ([Modern Indian School](https://misktm.edu.np/fee-structure/)). Scholarship handling also matters: a school of **500–800 students must reserve a 12% free-seat quota** ([eKantipur](https://ekantipur.com/Education/2026/04/06/under-what-headings-can-private-schools-charge-fees-13-47.html)). At about 700 students, Mikey is in that band, so discounts and scholarships need their own field on bills and their own report.

Real fee levels should go into mockups. The 2083 caps range from **Rs. 2,600 (Nursery) to Rs. 3,800 (Class 10)** in Buddhashanti, outside the valley ([Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/post/school-fees-buddha-shanti-rural-municipality/)), and **up to Rs. 7,755 for Class 10** in Budhanilkantha ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/04/26/kathmandu-municipalities-cap-private-school-fees)). Class names run Play Group, Nursery, LKG, UKG, then Class 1–10, with sections A/B/C. For a Banepa school, about Rs. 2,400 for Play Group rising to about Rs. 4,200 for Class 10 is a plausible inference. **No Banepa caps were found.** For sample data, Banepa has **14 wards**, and its population is mostly Newar (28%), Hill Brahmin (24%), Chhetri (20%) and Tamang (14%) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banepa)). That suggests surnames such as Shrestha, Maharjan, Adhikari, Poudel, Karki, Thapa, Tamang and Lama, and addresses in the form "Banepa-7, Kavrepalanchok". Name fields must not require a fixed first/last structure, and must accept middle names such as "Bahadur" or "Prasad" and abbreviations such as "K.C.".

### Receipts are PAN bills under computerized-billing rules

Education is in the VAT Act's exempt Schedule 1 ([Samriddhi Foundation](https://repository.samriddhi.org/paying-taxes/federal-taxes/value-added-tax/)), so the receipt has no 13% VAT line. The **3% Education Equality Fee** introduced from 1 Shrawan 2083 was **fully waived by Cabinet on 22 Jul 2026** ([Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/government-fully-waives-education-and-health-equality-fees/)), so no tax line is needed this year. The tax configuration should still allow one, because such levies come and go. The IRD's 2072 computerized-invoicing procedure matters more for the UI. It requires **chronological invoice numbers, cancellation with a reason rather than deletion, and reprints marked "copy of original" with a print count** ([Pioneer Law](https://pioneerlaw.com/?p=551)). Cancelled invoices must stay viewable and printable ([Frappe forum](https://discuss.frappe.io/t/compliance-requirement-that-must-be-fulfilled-to-get-approval-from-tax-authority-in-nepal/34532)). Parents may also register bills in IRD's bill-prize scheme, launched 2 Aug 2026. That scheme relies on a **9-digit seller PAN printed at the top**, a bill number, a date and a total of at least Rs. 100 ([PriceNepal](https://www.pricenepal.com/prize-ird-gov-np-register-bill-nepal/)). This makes the PAN placement practical as well as formal.

| Receipt zone | Contents |
|---|---|
| Header | Logo, school name, approval line, "Banepa-X, Kavre", phone, **PAN No.**, title "FEE RECEIPT" |
| Meta | Receipt No. (per-FY sequence), BS date (AD secondary), FY 2083/84, Session 2083 |
| Student | Name, Adm./Roll No., Class & Section, guardian, billing month(s) e.g. "Kartik 2083" |
| Body | S.N. · Particulars (legal fee head) · Amount (Rs.); then Sub-total, Discount/Scholarship, Previous dues, Late fine, **Total, Paid, Balance** |
| Words | "Rupees … Only" |
| Payment | Mode plus reference (cheque no./bank, voucher no., wallet or QR transaction ID) |
| Footer | Received by (cashier name and signature), stamp area, refund note; reprints watermarked "COPY OF ORIGINAL – Print #2" |

### Payment modes are cash plus five digital rails

Parents pay through **Khalti** (wallet, mobile banking, ConnectIPS, cards) ([Khalti](https://khalti.com/school-fee-payment/)), **eSewa** ([eSewa](https://blog.esewa.com.np/?p=1366)), **Fonepay QR**, which claims 99% of Nepal's person-to-merchant QR market ([Fonepay](https://fonepay.com/blogs/qr-payment-in-nepal)), bank deposit and cheque. Following Fedena's pattern, every mode except cash should require a **reference number**, and receipts should be searchable by it ([Fedena](https://support.fedena.com/support/solutions/articles/211460-about-fee-transaction-reference-number)). Cheques need a number, bank, date and clearance status. Bank deposits need a voucher number. Wallet and QR payments need a transaction ID. A sensible default order for the dropdown is Cash, Fonepay QR, Bank deposit, eSewa, Khalti, Mobile banking, ConnectIPS, Cheque. That order is a judgement, since no data on the payment mix in Nepali schools was found.

## The fee counter should feel like Tally, not a web form

Nepali accountants already know TallyPrime, which opens voucher types with single function keys (F5 Payment, F6 Receipt). Its stated principle is that **no essential function needs the mouse**. Tally also claims experienced users post **200+ vouchers per hour**, though that is a vendor claim, not a study ([Tally Solutions](https://tallysolutions.com/business-guides/tallyprime-keyboard-first-design-fast-data-entry/)). Tally shows its shortcuts (Ctrl+A accept, Esc back, Alt+P print, F2 change date) in a visible bottom bar ([TallyPrime shortcuts](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/welcome-to-tally-prime/keyboard-shortcuts-tally-prime)). Mikey's counter should do the same. Copying this idiom also makes a shadcn app read as a professional tool rather than a generic web page.

At 1366×768, the recommended layout has two panes. The **left pane (~40%)** holds student search and a student card: photo, class/section, roll number, guardian phone, and a large "Outstanding: Rs. X". The **right pane (~60%)** holds a fee-head table. Each row has a checkbox, head, BS month, due, paid, and an editable "Paying now" amount that defaults to the full due. EdPrime handles partial payments the same way: you tick each component, enter a partial amount, and the remainder carries forward ([EdPrime](https://edprime.zohodesk.in/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-collect-partial-payment)). Below the table sit mode, reference, tendered, change and remarks. A sticky footer shows the total and one primary button, **"Collect & Print (Ctrl+Enter)"**. PeopleSoft Campus Solutions uses the same model of target lines (what is paid) and tender lines (how it is paid), with a combined "Create and Print Receipt" commit. It also has a weakness to avoid: **totals update only when the user clicks Refresh** ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/_E80531_01/cs92pbr3/eng/cs/lssf/task_ProcessingCashieringTransactions-ab4f70.html)). Mikey's totals, change and balance should recalculate on every keystroke. With only ~700 students, the whole roster can sit in memory, so search by name, admission number, guardian phone or class is instant and survives brief LAN blips. The keyboard flow runs like this: F2 focuses search, then Enter selects a student, Space toggles a head, Tab moves to its amount, and Ctrl+Enter opens a review modal. That modal restates the student, heads, amount in figures and words, and mode, with the receipt preview in the same view.

Duplicate protection is a hard requirement, not a polish item. **Disabling the submit button alone does not stop duplicates.** The UI must set a submitting flag that also catches Enter-key submits and reset it on error. The real safeguard is a **server-side idempotency key** created when the form opens ([OpenReplay](https://blog.openreplay.com/prevent-double-form-submissions/)). Square's forum records a double-click that created a duplicate charge ([Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Archived-Discussions-Read-Only/Double-click-in-SqPaymentForm-created-a-duplicate-charge/td-p/72741)). On a retry, the server should return the original receipt, and the UI should say "Receipt #123 already saved" rather than show an error. **Receipt numbers must be assigned by the server**, never by either client PC, or the two counters will clash.

Every day should end on a **Day Close** screen. It shows the session's totals by payment mode, takes a counted-cash input, and displays the variance. This follows school cash-handling procedure, which requires end-of-day counts by payment type, reconciliation against a session report, and individual (never shared) logins ([Tasmania DoE](https://publicdocumentcentre.education.tas.gov.au/library/Document%20Centre/Cash-Handling-Procedure.pdf)). The same procedure says one person should not both receipt and reconcile. Where a small office cannot separate those duties, **a manager spot-checks at least four times a year** ([Tasmania DoE](https://publicdocumentcentre.education.tas.gov.au/library/Document%20Centre/Cash-Handling-Procedure.pdf)). With one Accountant and one Super Admin, that maps to a Super Admin review of each day close, or of periodic samples.

## Posted money never changes: void, approve, lock and log

QuickBooks shows the two options clearly. **Void keeps the record and zeroes it. Delete removes it everywhere except the audit log and leaves a permanent gap in the numbers** ([QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/customer-topics/void-or-delete-an-invoice-bill-or-other-transactions/00/186433)). Nepal's IRD rules forbid deleting recorded invoices, so Mikey should **expose no delete for posted receipts in either role**. The receipt detail page should be read-only, with two actions: Reprint, watermarked with a print count, and **Request void**. When an Accountant requests a void, they pick a required reason (Wrong student, Wrong amount, Duplicate, Cheque bounced, Other) and add free text. The receipt then shows **"Void pending"** but stays fully counted until the Super Admin approves it from a badge-count Approvals queue. PeopleSoft uses the same Pending status when approval is required ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/_E80531_01/cs92pbr3/eng/cs/lssf/task_ProcessingCashieringTransactions-ab4f70.html)). School procedure likewise requires manager approval for reversals and keeps the incorrect receipt, the reason and the approval evidence ([Tasmania DoE](https://publicdocumentcentre.education.tas.gov.au/library/Document%20Centre/Cash-Handling-Procedure.pdf)). An approved void reopens the student's dues and links both ways to any re-issued receipt. Lists show voided receipts with a "Void" chip and a "Show voided" toggle. They are never hidden.

A **period lock** completes the controls. Zoho Books' transaction locking prevents editing anything dated before a set date and restricts back-dated entries ([Zoho Books](https://www.zoho.com/au/books/kb/accountant/acc-transactionlock.html)). Mikey's Super Admin should set "Books locked up to [BS date]", for example the end of each BS month after reconciliation. Date pickers on entry forms then disable locked dates and show a tooltip giving the reason. Corrections go in the current period and are never backdated ([Beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/16/void-vs-delete-quickbooks-audit-trail-guide)).

The **audit log** should follow the model used by current platforms. Each entry records actor, action, resource, result, timestamp and interface. Users can filter on each of these dimensions, click any cell to include or exclude matching entries, and open a **side-by-side diff of changed fields** ([Cloudflare changelog](https://codex-container-api-docs.previews.developers.cloudflare.com/logs/changelog/audit-logs/); a preview-domain copy, so treat specifics as provisional). For Mikey, the modules are Fees, Attendance, Devices, Backup, Settings and Users. Each entry should also record the workstation, since there are two PCs. The log must be append-only, exportable to CSV/PDF for inspections, and must mark **voids, restores, device-log clears, rule changes and fee-structure edits as high-severity**. Each record should also carry its own activity timeline, so the accountant sees the history without opening the global log.

## Navigation, connection status and dashboards differ by role

### Hide what the Accountant will never use, and explain anything disabled

A clear test decides between hiding and disabling: **"Will a given user ever be able to interact with this element?"** If not, hide it. Admin-only controls are the textbook case. Anything disabled must say why and how to enable it ([Smashing Magazine](https://smashingmagazine.com/2024/05/hidden-vs-disabled-ux/); [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/why-disabled-buttons-hurt-ux-and-how-to-fix-them/)). The Accountant's sidebar should therefore show Dashboard, Collect Fee, Students (read-only), Receipts, Dues/Defaulters, Day Close, Attendance (view and correct, if the client agrees) and limited Reports. Users & Roles, Fee Structure, School Settings, Devices, Backup/Restore, Audit Log and Period Lock should be **absent**, not greyed out. State-based limits inside visible screens should be disabled with an explanation, for example "Books locked up to 31 Ashwin 2083". Void becomes "Request void" rather than a dead button. Mikey has only two fixed roles, so a full role × permission matrix editor ([Dash](https://brand.dash.fi/patterns/role-permission-matrix)) is likely overkill. A **read-only matrix plus a few toggles** suits it better, such as "Accountant may apply discounts" or "Accountant may backdate up to N days". The server must enforce every rule, because hiding something in the UI is not security.

### A heartbeat, not the browser, decides whether the server is reachable

MDN calls `navigator.onLine` **"inherently unreliable"**. It reports "online" on a LAN that has no reachable service, and it advises using it only for hints ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Navigator/onLine)). Instead, the Electron main process should ping the office server's health endpoint every 5–10 seconds. It should treat two or three consecutive failures as offline, back off retries, and broadcast the state to every window. A persistent indicator in the status bar should show icon plus text: **"Connected · Server: OFFICE-PC"**, "Reconnecting… (attempt 3)", or "Offline – changes cannot be saved". The chrome should also change color so the state is recognizable on any screen, and a last-sync time should be visible. Both patterns come from SAP Fiori's offline guidance ([SAP](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-ios/v25-8/components/feedback/offline/usage)). When the server is unreachable, read screens keep showing cached data under an amber banner. The Collect button is disabled with an inline reason, and whatever the user has entered stays in the form. If a commit fails midway, a blocking modal offers "Retry (safe, won't duplicate)", which the idempotency key makes safe. **No receipt prints until the server confirms the save.** Queuing payments offline would break sequential, server-assigned numbering across two counters. "Offline-first" should therefore mean the system runs entirely without the internet, while money writes always need the LAN server.

### Two dashboards for two jobs

Operational dashboards default to "today", lead with **3–5 large numbers, each with one comparison**, and end in an action list. Analytical dashboards default to longer periods and rely on filters and drill-downs ([Basedash](https://www.basedash.com/blog/operational-dashboards-vs-analytical-dashboards-how-to-design-each)). The **Accountant's dashboard** fits above the fold at 1366×768. It has four tiles (Collected today vs yesterday, Receipts today, Expected cash in drawer, Pending void requests), then today's collections by payment mode and a Recent receipts list with reprint. "Collect fee (F2)" stays pinned in the header. The **Super Admin's dashboard** shows collected this BS month and this session, expected vs collected with a progress bar, total outstanding, and students overdue. Below those come charts: a monthly collection trend by BS month, outstanding by class sorted descending, and collection by fee head. Top defaulters and the approvals queue follow. Date presets should use Nepali periods (Today, This BS month, This session, FY 2083/84, Custom), and every tile should open its filtered list.

## Dense tables and tabular figures give the app its professional look

With ~700 records, every table can load its full dataset and filter or sort instantly on the client. Carbon's density scale gives **32px compact and 40px default rows**. Toolbars should have **at most five actions**, with the rest in an overflow menu, and column titles should be one or two words in sentence case ([Carbon](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/)). Text should be left-aligned and amounts right-aligned, with the header pinned on vertical scroll and the identifier column pinned on horizontal scroll ([UX Collective](https://bootcamp.uxdesign.cc/data-table-design-patterns-4e38188a0981)). At 1366×768, 32–36px rows show roughly 15–17 records. A compact/comfortable toggle saved per user covers both preferences. The student dues list should keep to about eight columns: Adm. No (sticky), Name, Class–Sec, Guardian phone, Total due, Paid, Outstanding, Last payment (BS), and a status chip such as Paid / Partial / Overdue. Add a pinned totals footer, a persistent filter bar with removable chips, and a "Showing 128 of 702" count. Virtual scrolling suits search-and-act lists, and pagination at 50–100 rows suits printable reports.

For typography, apply **`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to every money column** so digits line up ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/font-variant-numeric)). Inter, IBM Plex Sans (with clear 0/O and 1/l distinction) and Source Sans 3 are all strong numeral fonts ([FontAlternatives](https://fontalternatives.com/blog/best-fonts-legible-numerals-ui/)). For Devanagari, **Mukta** covers Devanagari plus Latin in 7 weights, includes the `tnum` feature, and is OFL-licensed ([Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/fonts/mukta/about)). Noto Sans Devanagari is the fallback ([Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/fonts/noto-sans-devanagari/about)), though its tabular support was not verified. A stack of `"Inter", "Mukta", "Noto Sans Devanagari", system-ui` covers both scripts. Fonts must be **bundled in the Electron app** (for example via `@fontsource`), because LAN PCs may have no internet to reach a font CDN.

For color, keep text at **4.5:1 contrast** and UI components and chart marks at **3:1**. Ratios cannot be rounded up: #777777 on white (4.47:1) fails ([WebAIM](https://webaim.org/articles/contrast/); [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/non-text-contrast)). Color must never be the only cue ([WebAIM](https://webaim.org/articles/contrast/)). Keep the chrome neutral with one accent, and reserve teal/green for paid, amber for partial, and red for overdue/void/error. Status chips always carry text. Ledgers show money in and out through Debit/Credit columns and signs, not color alone. Dash's teal-up and orange-down pairing also helps red-green color-blind users ([Dash](https://brand.dash.fi/foundations/data-viz)). Departing from shadcn's default zinc-and-black theme is a judgement call rather than a sourced rule. Practical ways to do it are a non-default neutral, hairline borders instead of shadows, large semibold tabular totals, a Tally-style shortcut footer, and a muted custom Recharts palette.

## Biometric attendance needs a raw-punch layer before any register

### Device records, connection tests and sync share one model across vendors

Vendors model devices the same way. **Anviz CrossChex** records a terminal number, name and LAN/USB mode, plus IP, **port 5010** and a communication password. Its **"Test Connect" also syncs the device clock with the PC**, and its downloads split into **"DL New Records" and "DL All Records"** ([Anviz](https://www.anviz.com/file/download/10285/CrossChexLiteUserGuideENV1.2.pdf)). **ZKTeco** devices use **port 4370** and a communication key that must match the device setting. Vendors advise a static IP ([Enerpize](https://docs.enerpize.com/?p=5318)). The ZK protocol also exposes destructive calls such as `clearAttendance()` ([ZKLibrary](https://github.com/kamshory/ZKLibrary)). In push (ADMS) mode, the device reports its own serial and IP, and its status turns green once confirmed ([ZKTeco BioTime guide](https://cdn.document360.io/0b8a1602-1fb0-4465-a168-a93a81974a0b/Images/Documentation/Gu%C3%ADa%20Rapida_Agregar%20dispositivo%20BioTime%20Pro.pdf)).

The brand is not yet confirmed, so the Devices screen should support **TCP pull, ADMS push and USB file import** in one list. Its columns are Name, Location, Serial (read-only), IP:Port, Method, a status chip (Online / Offline / Unreachable / Disabled), Last contact, Last download (time and count), Enrolled users, and Clock drift. "Test connection" should report each stage separately: reachable, key accepted, serial read, clock read. "Clear device logs" belongs in an overflow menu and appears only after a confirmed download. Sync should show progress per device ("Reading 212 of 340 … Done: 312 new, 4 duplicates skipped, 2 unmatched") and keep a run history. QHRM's 2026 fixes show what goes wrong in practice: logs that showed the device IP instead of its name, timestamps in server time instead of local time, and a **retry flood after a network outage**. Its fix list also adds device-offline alerts ([QHRM](https://qhrm.io/resources/release-notes/v5-1-attendance-sync)). The eSSL integration syncs **every 15 minutes** by default and also offers a manual date-range sync ([Odoo eSSL app](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/de_essl_etimetracklite_integration)).

Unmatched and duplicate punches must be kept, never dropped. Odoo's BioTime integration matches punches by device employee ID. A punch that matches no one keeps a **"Processing Note" and stays unprocessed until someone fixes the mapping and clicks "Process"** ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/master/applications/hr/attendances/biotime_integration.html)). Mikey should have an **Unmatched IDs inbox** grouped by enrollment ID, showing punch count, first and last seen, and a "Map to student/staff" action that reprocesses the held punches. The raw punch log is read-only and kept apart from computed attendance, as the eSSL integration does by storing punches "before it is interpreted" ([Odoo eSSL app](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/de_essl_etimetracklite_integration)). Each punch carries a processing status: Processed, Duplicate, Unmatched, Outside window, or Ignored. Repeat taps are suppressed by a **duplicate-punch window** ([CAMS](https://forum.camsbiometrics.com/optimizing-attendance-accuracy-understanding-and-managing-duplicate-punch-duration-in-biometric-systems/)) or a "Min. Interval" ([Hikvision](https://enpinfo.hikvision.com/hkwsen/unzip/20230830144955_12918_doc/GUID-6E4A3479-16B1-42E8-9924-5B12646AAD79.html)). No source gives a default value, so it must be configurable. Suppressed punches stay in the log, labelled Duplicate.

### Registers use one-letter codes, and corrections sit as an overlay

School software shows attendance as a **students × days matrix with single-letter codes and per-student totals**, filtered by class and month, with print and Excel export ([openSIS](https://help.opensis.com/portal/en/kb/articles/generate-student-monthly-attendance-register)). Vendors' codes clash. Smart School uses H for Holiday and F for Half Day ([Smart School](https://smart-school.in/article/how-to-check-student-yearly-session-attendance-in-his-profile-page-from-the-superadmin-side)), while openSIS uses H for Half Day. Mikey therefore needs an unambiguous code set with a legend that is always visible.

| Code | Meaning | Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | Present | I | Incomplete (single punch, pending) |
| A | Absent | LV | Approved leave |
| L | Late | H | Holiday |
| E | Early leave | W | Weekly off (Saturday, to confirm) |
| HD | Half day | – | No school day |

The **monthly matrix** follows BS months with 29–32 columns, keeps the roll number and name columns sticky, shades off-days, and totals P/A/L/LV plus percentage over working days. Clicking a cell opens its raw punches and edit history. It prints on A4 landscape. The **daily register** filters by date, class and section. It shows first in, last out, status, minutes late and source (Biometric / Manual / Import), with clickable counters for Present, Absent, Late, Incomplete and Not yet arrived. A class-wise summary lists strength, present, absent, late and percentage. Single-punch days stay **Incomplete** until day close and then resolve by rule. Hikvision's "No Check-Out, Mark as Absent/Early Leave" works the same way ([Hikvision](https://enpinfo.hikvision.com/hkwsen/unzip/20230830144955_12918_doc/GUID-808B72B4-D3F9-4F3D-B955-C3D0BE1AB4B4.html)). Manual corrections never touch raw punches. A dialog records old value, new value, a required reason, and who and when. The corrected cell carries a visible marker, and the change goes to the audit log.

### Rule settings should be drawn, not just typed

Hikvision documents the most complete rule model. It covers scheduled start and end times, **valid check-in and check-out windows**, a **late/early allowance (grace)**, minimum hours, "First In & Last Out" calculation, and day-change time ([Hikvision timetable](https://enpinfo.hikvision.com/hkwsen/unzip/20230830144955_12918_doc/GUID-6E4A3479-16B1-42E8-9924-5B12646AAD79.html)). It also has "Absent If Check-In Late" thresholds ([Hikvision absence](https://enpinfo.hikvision.com/hkwsen/unzip/20230830144955_12918_doc/GUID-808B72B4-D3F9-4F3D-B955-C3D0BE1AB4B4.html)), and rules apply in order global, then department, then group, with the group rule winning ([Hikvision rules](https://enpinfo.hikvision.com/hkwsen/unzip/20230830144955_12918_doc/GUID-E5BC3C52-CF65-454A-90C9-1A9EC3913A77.html)). Mikey's form should use school language. Its fields are School opens, Check-in window starts, Grace (min), Absent if after, Early departure before, School closes, Day close (when Incomplete resolves and the day locks), If no check-out, and Ignore repeat punches within N min. Rules would be set per profile (students vs staff), with a school-wide default. Hikvision's "Timetable Overview" suggests a **live timeline with colored bands** for the valid, grace, late and absent zones, which lets a non-technical admin check the rules at a glance. Any edit should prompt "Rules changed – recalculate from [BS date]?".

## Backups must prove themselves, and restore must be hard to do by accident

Mature backup tools share a common set of features. They have a schedule with an **integrity-check option** and client-side encryption, a retention policy, and a **timeline slider for picking a version**, with a warning before anything is overwritten ([WunderTech on Synology Hyper Backup](https://www.wundertech.net/synology-hyper-backup/)). Status rolls up with **Error taking precedence over Warning over OK** ([Acronis](https://www.acronis.com/en/support/documentation/AcronisBackup_11.5/3687.html)), and a run history opens each session's statistics ([Veeam](https://helpcenter.veeam.com/docs/vbr/userguide/session_results_web.html)). Mikey's Backup screen should therefore open with a status header ("Last successful backup: today 17:05 · uploaded to cloud 17:07", next run, Back up now, overall chip). Below it come schedule settings: daily time or on app close, how many local copies to keep, cloud retention, an encryption passphrase with a warning that losing it makes backups unrecoverable, and a weekly verify. Then comes a history table with type (Scheduled / Manual / Pre-restore), size, local and cloud badges, checksum verified, and status. Last is a pending-upload queue showing the error, the next retry with backoff, and "Retry now". Cost is no barrier to an offsite copy. Cloudflare R2's free tier gives **10 GB-month storage with free egress**, and beyond that storage costs **$0.015/GB-month** ([Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/index.md)). That is far more than a 700-student database needs.

Restore is the most destructive action in the app, so it gets the strongest confirmation available. Design guidance reserves **type-to-confirm** for catastrophic, permanent actions. The confirmation should name the exact loss, use verb buttons, and never auto-focus the destructive one ([SaaS UI](https://www.saasui.design/blog/saas-destructive-actions-confirmation-ux-patterns)). The restore flow has six steps. The user picks a version, then previews its record counts (students, receipts, attendance days). Next comes plain consequence text: "Everything entered after 12 Oct 17:05 will be replaced; 23 receipts and 4 attendance days would be lost". The app then takes an **automatic pre-restore backup**, shown as a visible step, and the user confirms by typing "RESTORE" or the school name. Last, the app restarts with a banner offering "Undo restore". Restore belongs to the Super Admin only and is always logged as high-severity. The restore warning matters because restoring wipes receipts that may already have been printed and handed to parents. That raises a question the client must answer: can a restored database reuse receipt numbers that were already issued?

## Conclusion

Nepal's regulations settle many design decisions that would otherwise be judgement calls. The 14 legal fee headings become a fixed list in the data model, and the over-limit warnings become compliance features. IRD's ban on deleting invoices turns "void, never delete" from good practice into a legal requirement. The BS calendar affects more than display: 32-day months break date validation, matrix layouts and any range logic copied from Gregorian libraries. The less obvious finding is a conflict inside the brief. "Offline-first" and "two counters with sequential, server-assigned receipt numbers" cannot both apply to money. The way through is to define offline as independence from the internet, not from the office server, and to make the connection indicator, idempotent commits and blocked writes the visible sign of that decision.

The biggest risks are the unknowns, not the UI patterns. The device brand decides whether attendance ingestion is a TCP client, an HTTP listener or a file importer. The printer decides the receipt's form factor. IRD certification could add formal requirements to numbering and reprints. The Banepa fee caps decide what the fee-structure validator enforces. Settling those four questions with the client before screen design starts will save more rework than any visual refinement.
